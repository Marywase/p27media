About the Founder

Jonas Emmanuel

Jonas brings over a decade of experience capturing unforgettable moments on film. Before focusing on videography, he mastered the art of managing high-profile trade shows and events in the fashion industry — blending creativity, precision, and a sharp eye for detail to bring every story vividly to life.

The Inspiration

From the vibrant chaos of fashion trade shows to the quiet focus of a camera lens, Jonas's journey has always centered around one thing: storytelling.

Before becoming a full-time filmmaker, Jonas spent years developing his unique approach to cinematic storytelling, where every second mattered and every detail had to be perfect.

The camera became more than a tool—it revealed Jonas’s true calling. Inspired by a deeper creative pull, he stepped fully into videography.

For more than a decade, Jonas has shaped unforgettable stories — capturing weddings, documentaries, and branded pieces with a signature cinematic touch

Today, Jonas leads P27MEDIA, where his mission is clear: to help others tell their stories with truth, heart, and cinematic excellence.

“Feel embraced as you step into the gallery, where every frame brings stories to life.
